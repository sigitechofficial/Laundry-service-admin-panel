The business in one page

Just Dry Cleaners is a laundry and dry-cleaning marketplace in the UK. A customer books a collection in the app. A local partner laundry shop accepts the order, collects the bag, counts and prices the items at the shop, cleans them and delivers them back. The platform takes care of booking, payment, routing the order to the right shop and supporting everyone involved.

Customer app

Book a collection, choose services and preferences, pay, track the driver, answer questions about items, rate the shop, manage repeat orders.

Shop app

Used by the partner shop owner and staff: accept orders, collect, invoice, clean, deliver, manage the team, see earnings and hand over cash.

Admin panel

Used by the operations team: watch every order, move orders between shops, manage customers, shops, zones, prices, policies, promotions, payouts and reports.

The words everyone uses

Zone
An area (a group of postcodes on the map) with its own minimum order, service fee, shop share and services. Every customer address and every shop belongs to one zone.
Slot
A one-hour window for collection or delivery, for example 11:00–12:00.
Minimum order
The least an order costs in a zone. Card customers pay it when they book; it is counted towards their laundry.
Service fee
A fixed fee per order that belongs to the platform. Promotions never discount it (on cash orders, customer credit can still cover it).
Pay now
What a card customer pays when booking: minimum order + service fee + driver tip.
Invoice
The final bill the shop makes after counting and pricing the items. Promotions are worked out here.
Left to pay
Invoice total, minus what was paid at booking, minus discounts. Never below £0, and nothing paid at booking is refunded.
Preferred shop
The shop a returning customer used before (or the one admin assigned). It gets the customer's new order first, for a few minutes, before other shops.
Broadcast
Offering an order to every suitable shop in the zone at once. The first shop to accept gets it.
On hold
The shop found a problem with an item (damage, stain, wrong item) and is waiting for the customer to say what to do.
Remittance
Cash a shop collected from customers that belongs to the platform, handed over and confirmed by admin.
Promotion
An offer (percentage off, money off, cashback, first-order discount and so on). Automatic, or unlocked with a coupon code.
Credit
Money in the customer's account (from cashback or a goodwill gesture) that is used automatically on their next invoice.
The one rule to rememberCustomers book without final prices. The shop counts and prices the laundry after collection, so the real bill, every discount and the amount left to pay are decided on the shop's invoice, not at booking.

An order from start to finish

This is the life of one order. The labels in grey boxes are the status names the shop and admin see.

  1. Customer

    Books a collection

    Chooses address, collection slot, delivery slot, services, bags, preferences, tip, payment method and how often (once, weekly, every 2 or every 4 weeks). A card customer authorises "Pay now"; the card is held, not charged yet. Order Created

  2. Platform

    Finds a shop

    The preferred shop gets the order alone for a few minutes (default 10). If it does not accept, or declines, every suitable shop in the zone is offered it and the first to accept wins. If no shop can take it, the order waits and admin is alerted. See How an order finds a shop. Confirmed Awaiting Collection

  3. Shop

    Collects the laundry

    The driver taps "I'm on the way" (the card hold is now charged), arrives, and collects the bags. If nobody answers, the driver records a failed attempt and a no-show fee may apply. Driver Out for PickUp Driver Reached Pickup PickingUp and Inspection In Transit to Facility

  4. Shop

    Counts, prices and invoices

    At the shop the items are counted and priced from the catalogue. Promotions and credit are applied. The shop prints garment tags. If an item has a problem, the order goes on hold until the customer answers. Delivered Laundry to Shop Agent Added the Services Invoice Generated On Hold/Waiting for customer response

  5. Platform

    Takes the balance

    Card: what is left to pay is charged automatically, by default 2 hours after the invoice is finalised, so the customer can see the bill first. Cash: the driver collects it at the door. A failed card charge goes to the admin's Payment failures list.

  6. Shop

    Cleans

    Washing, dry cleaning, ironing and repairs are done. Processing Completed (At Facility)

  7. Shop

    Delivers

    A driver is assigned, the customer can follow the driver on the map, and the bags are handed over. If delivery fails, it is rescheduled. Out for Delivery Driver Reached Delivery Failed Delivered

  8. Everyone

    Completed

    The shop's earning is booked, cashback becomes credit, and the customer is asked to rate the shop. A repeat order is created by itself on schedule. Completed

Money: what the customer pays

Card orders

  • At booking: the card is authorised for "Pay now" = minimum order + service fee + driver tip. Nothing is taken yet.
  • When the driver sets off to collect: that authorised amount is charged.
  • If the card hold has run out by the time the driver sets off, "I'm on the way" makes a fresh charge instead. If that fails, the driver cannot set off until the payment is sorted.
  • After the invoice: what is left to pay is charged to the same card automatically, by default 2 hours after the invoice is finalised. The shop should finalise the invoice within 1 hour of collection (the shop app shows a countdown).
  • Retries: only temporary declines are tried again (not enough money, "try again later", bank processing error), up to 3 tries, 30 minutes apart. A hard decline (expired, lost or stolen card, "do not honour", or the bank asking for 3-D Secure) goes straight to Payment failures after the first try.
  • The customer can fix it themselves: when they add a new card or choose another default card, every open card order moves to that card, the failure is cleared and the order leaves Payment failures. It is charged again straight away only while automatic tries are left; once all 3 tries are used up, the order goes straight back to Payment failures and admin decides.
  • No payment, no delivery: when the driver taps "Out for delivery" on an unpaid card order, one more charge is tried on the spot. If it fails, the driver sees "Please wait for admin instruction. Payment still needs to be processed." until admin decides.
  • Minimum order: the minimum paid at booking counts towards the laundry. If the laundry comes to less than the minimum, the difference is not refunded.

Cash orders

  • Nothing is paid at booking.
  • At delivery the driver collects: laundry (at least the minimum order; a "Minimum order top-up" line is added if the laundry is below it) + service fee + tip, minus discounts and credit.
  • No-show fees cannot be charged on cash orders (there is no card to charge).

How the bill reads

Real example (order 1802-449217): card order, laundry £89, service fee £10, a "£50 off each item" promotion, minimum order £20.

Laundry£89.00
Service fee£10.00
Total before discount£99.00
Discount−£69.00
Total after discount£30.00
Paid at booking−£30.00
Left to pay£0.00

The offer was worth £89 (every item free), but a discount can only take off what is still to pay. £30 was paid at booking (minimum order £20 + service fee £10), so the discount is £69. It is shared across the items in proportion to their prices.

Who earns what on one order

PendingA different split for a customer's first three orders (1st: 100% Admin; 2nd: 50% Admin, 50% Agent; 3rd: 100% Shop; then normal rates) is planned but not built. See Pending work.
  • Shop: the zone's shop share (a percentage, for example 70% or 80%) of the laundry, plus all tips. For cash orders the share is on the laundry or the minimum order, whichever is higher. On card orders where the laundry is below the minimum, the share is worked out on the laundry only and the platform keeps the minimum-order top-up.
  • Platform: the rest of the laundry plus the whole service fee.
  • Discounts and credit are paid by the platform. The shop's share is worked out on the price before discount, so promotions never reduce what the shop earns.
  • The zone's minimum order, service fee and shop share are fixed on the order when a shop accepts it. Changing the zone later does not change orders already accepted.

Refunds

  • Automatic: when a customer cancels after the card was charged, the system refunds "Pay now" minus the cancellation fee to the card straight away.
  • Everything else is refunded by admin from the order page, with a reason: Full remaining or a Partial amount.
  • Admin does not choose the method: card payments are refunded to the card first (via Stripe, a few working days), then any remaining amount that was paid in cash is shown as "Cash (manual)" for staff to hand back. The refund window shows this split and how much of the shop's commission is taken back.
  • The customer sees a "Refund issued" or "Partial refund issued" banner on the order, with the reason. Card: "on its way back to your card". Cash: "Collect £X cash from the shop or driver."
  • The shop's earning is reduced in proportion to the share refunded.
  • A full refund marks the order Refunded, gives the promotion's use and campaign budget back, takes back unspent cashback and returns credit spent on that order.

All the rules at a glance

Every business rule on one page, for explaining the platform to someone new. Each group links to the full detail. Amounts marked "zone setting" are set per zone by admin in Policies.

Orders and shops

  • An order only goes to shops in the customer's zone. The address decides the zone; an address outside every zone cannot be booked.
  • A customer's first order goes to every suitable shop in the zone at once, nearest first. The first shop to accept gets it.
  • After a shop completes one order for a customer, that shop gets the customer's next orders first, alone, for 10 minutes (Runtime checks). Admin calls a customer "Returning" at a shop after 2 completed orders there.
  • If admin assigns a preferred shop, only that shop gets the head-start; the old shop competes like everyone else.
  • If admin excludes a shop for a customer (bad experience), that shop never gets the customer's orders. A bad review alone does not exclude a shop; admin decides.
  • A shop never sees an order it declined, an order for services it does not offer, or an order for a time it is closed, on hold or full.
  • Timeline: preferred shop alone for 10 minutes (the switch can take up to 5 minutes more), then every suitable shop for 30 minutes. An offer that runs out of time is not sent again; admin assigns it. Detail: Timeline of one order.
  • Declining needs a reason and does not cancel the order. If the preferred shop declines, the order goes to everyone at once.
  • An order only goes to shops in the customer's zone; inside the zone there is no distance limit, only nearest-first order.
  • If no shop can take an order, the customer is not refused: the order waits and admin gets "Order needs manual assignment". Admin can assign any shop, even a full one; an excluded shop asks "Assign anyway?" first.
  • Business hours: a shop is offered an order only if the collection slot is inside its hours on the collection day, and only while it is open or has its app open. Orders booked while every shop is closed, or at night, are held and sent when shops open. After a shop accepts, hours no longer limit the order. Detail: Business hours.
  • Full shops are left out: when slot capacity is on, a shop whose collection or delivery slot is full gets no notification, no sound and does not see the order; it goes to the shops with room. If every shop is full, the order waits, admin is told, and it is sent again when a slot frees up. Detail: When a shop's slot is full.
  • Slot capacity is off by default (no limit). When it is on, each shop takes up to 4 orders per one-hour slot (or the number admin sets), counting pickups and deliveries.

Booking

  • Only open slots are shown. Shops can only open inside the platform's operational hours.
  • Collection slots start at least 2 hours from now (rounded up to the hour) and run 7 days ahead. Delivery slots start at least 24 hours out and run 21 days ahead.
  • Delivery must be at least the slowest chosen service's turnaround after collection. The app moves the delivery date itself ("Your delivery date has been updated to…") and the server refuses a booking that breaks this rule.
  • The customer's profile (name and phone) must be complete before booking.
  • Before saving wash preferences the customer must accept: "You are responsible if clothes colour bleeds due to the selected wash settings."
  • Guests can look around but must sign in to see the summary and pay.
  • A wrong coupon code shows the reason, and the order cannot be placed until the code is fixed or removed.

Payment

  • Card: at booking the card is only held for "Pay now" (minimum order + service fee + tip). It is charged when the driver taps "I'm on the way" to collect.
  • After the invoice, the rest is charged automatically, first try 2 hours later. Only temporary declines are retried (up to 3 tries, 30 minutes apart); a hard decline (expired or stolen card, 3-D Secure needed) goes straight to admin's Payment failures: Shift to Cash, Allow Proceed or Keep Waiting. Keep Waiting stops the automatic retries.
  • When the customer adds or picks another card, all their open card orders move to it and a failed payment is charged again straight away, as long as automatic tries are left; after 3 tries admin has to decide.
  • An unpaid card order cannot go out for delivery: one more charge is tried when the driver taps "Out for delivery", then the driver waits for admin.
  • Cash: nothing is paid at booking; the driver collects everything at delivery. If the laundry is below the minimum, a "Minimum order top-up" line is added. No cancellation, no-show or reschedule fees can be charged on cash orders.
  • If the laundry comes to less than the minimum order, the difference is not refunded.
  • No invoice, no delivery: an order cannot go out for delivery before it is invoiced.

Shop rules

  • A shop gets orders only after admin approves it, and only for orders whose services it all offers.
  • Declining does not cancel the order; the shop just never sees it again.
  • "I've arrived" checks the driver's location. "Proceed anyway" is allowed but recorded and reported to admin (Location compliance).
  • The owner must connect Stripe before using the app; the reminder cannot be closed.
  • Staff get a role: Manager (accept and assign jobs, manage the team) or Driver (run assigned jobs only). Wallet, bank, reports and business hours stay with the owner.
  • A photo is required to complete every collection and every delivery.
  • The shop should finalise the invoice within 1 hour of collection (the driver's drive-to-shop screen reminds them). The shop does not choose card or cash: card balances are charged by the platform, cash is collected at delivery.
  • Nobody on the team can log out while the shop has any active order (the app checks this); the owner cannot delete the account, or switch today to a day off, while the shop has active orders.
  • Cash stays with the shop until it hands it over ("Submit remittance"); admin confirms or rejects. Card earnings reach the shop's Stripe account when admin pays them out. The shop can request a withdrawal (minimum £1) only of money admin has already released to its wallet.
  • Item problem: the shop puts items on hold with photos and a note, the customer answers keep or remove per item, and the shop updates the invoice and carries on.

Customer rules

  • First-order offers are only for customers with no earlier order that was not cancelled (any status counts).
  • Offers are not taken off at checkout; they come off the invoice after the shop counts the items. "Pay now" never changes.
  • Cashback arrives as credit after delivery and is used automatically on the next invoice. Credit expires after 365 days.
  • One extra tip (£1–£200) can be added per order after delivery; all tips go to the shop (the booking screen's text still says the courier; see known issues).
  • A review needs at least one reason: 1–2 stars only negative reasons, 4–5 only positive, 3 either. Comment up to 500 characters.
  • Admin can block a customer only when they have no unfinished orders. Blocking signs them out and pauses their repeat plans (even if the block is refused); unblocking does not restart the plans.
  • Admin "Delete & anonymize" removes name and contacts but keeps order history.

Repeat orders and refunds

  • Repeat plans: weekly, every 2 or every 4 weeks. The plan starts only after the first booking succeeds. Each next order copies the address, slots, services (at today's prices) and goes to the preferred shop first.
  • The customer can pause, resume or turn off a plan; only admin changes how often. Turning off is permanent. 3 failures in a row pause the plan. Cancelling one order does not stop the plan.
  • Refunds: automatic when a customer cancels after paying (minus the fee); otherwise admin refunds in full or part, with a reason. Card payments go back to the card first, then any cash part is handed back by staff. The shop's earning drops in proportion. A full refund also returns the offer's use and campaign budget, takes back unspent cashback and gives back credit spent on the order.

Booking an order

  1. Where and when

    Pick a saved address or search a UK postcode ("Please enter a full UK postcode (e.g. SW10 0AA)"). Choose the address type, the collection date and slot and how to collect (in person, outside, reception or porter), then the delivery date and slot and how to deliver (in person, leave at the door, reception or porter). Only slots that are open are shown ("No time slots available for this day. Please select another date."). Collection: the next 7 days, from 2 hours ahead (today drops off after 7 pm). Delivery: the earliest slot is 24 hours after the chosen collection, or collection plus the slowest service's turnaround if that is later; the date list covers turnaround + 7 days (between 7 and 21 days). A free-text "Driver Instructions" box goes with the order. "Use my current location" and the last 5 addresses (with Clear) speed this up. An address outside every zone cannot be booked.

  2. What

    Tick the services (for example Dry Cleaning, Wash & Iron), the number of bags and items, and whether everything goes in one bag. Set preferences per service (temperature, detergent, softener, tumble dry, ironing, instructions). Before preferences are saved the customer must accept "You are responsible if clothes colour bleeds due to the selected wash settings." For repairs, choose the garment, the repair and quantity, and add up to 5 photos per garment. If bags or items are left empty the app asks "Go Back & Add" or "Proceed Anyway". If the delivery date is too soon for the slowest service's turnaround, the app moves it ("Your delivery date has been updated to…") and offers "Use suggested date" or "Change delivery date"; the server refuses a delivery that is too soon.

  3. Summary and payment

    Driver tip (£0, £1, £2, £5 or an own amount; the card's text says the tip goes "directly to the courier", although the system pays all tips to the shop), coupon code, offers on this order, credit note, payment by card or cash, and how often (Just once, Weekly, Every two weeks, Every four weeks). The total to pay now is minimum order + service fee + tip. The button reads "Pay Now" for card (the card is authorised with the card sheet) or "Book Now" for cash (nothing is paid now).

More on the summary screen

  • Card payment uses Stripe's card sheet, which also offers Apple Pay and Google Pay; later balance and fee charges go to that same method. Closing the sheet simply returns to the summary.
  • "Edit" on the services lets the customer remove one; the app explains the effect ("Service removed. Delivery date updated to… Processing time is now N days").
  • "How charges work?" tells the customer they pay the minimum order plus the service fee when placing the order, and the difference if the order costs more. The same text is shown for cash orders (which pay nothing at booking) and it does not mention the tip.
  • Tip and coupon: choosing or changing the tip removes a coupon code that was already applied, without a message. The code must be entered after the tip.
  • A custom booking tip has no upper limit (the extra tip after delivery is limited to £1–£200). Repeat orders copy the original tip.
  • Zones: services and repair options depend on the address's zone. Switching to an address in another zone silently drops services that zone does not offer.
  • Errors appear as "Payment Failed" or "Booking Failed" with the server's message. If repair photos cannot be uploaded the booking stops ("Could not upload repair photos").
Coupon codes at checkoutA valid code is saved on the order and the discount is taken off the invoice after the shop inspects the items. "Pay now" stays the same. A wrong code shows the reason (see Promotions) and nothing is booked until it is fixed or removed.

Shop app

The app for partner laundry shops. The owner sees everything. Staff are either Drivers or Managers and see only what their role allows. Drivers do not see prices, invoice totals, earnings or the customer's email, and the customer's phone number is masked.

Joining the platform

  1. Sign up

    Name, email, password and phone, then a 4-digit code sent by email (valid for 1 minute).

  2. Shop address

    UK postcode search and a pin on the map. The address decides the zone and the distances to customers.

  3. Business details

    Six short steps: shop name, services offered, working days and hours (inside the platform's opening hours), type of business, number of machines, and turnaround time per service.

  4. Stripe payouts

    Connect a Stripe account to receive card earnings. This is required for the owner: until Stripe is connected, a "Stripe Account Not Connected" window appears every time the main screen opens. It cannot be closed; the only button is "Verify & Connect". Staff never see it.

  5. Approval

    The shop waits on "Admin approval pending" (the screen says this "typically takes 1–2 business days") until admin approves it in Shop Management → Onboarding Requests.

Owner and staff log in differently: the login screen has an "Agent" / "Employee" switch. Staff use Employee; they skip shop set-up and Stripe. A staff account trying the owner login is told to use Employee login.

Collecting

  1. "I'm on the way": the customer's card is charged for "Pay now" and the customer can follow the driver on the map. If the card hold has run out, a fresh charge is made; if it fails, the driver cannot set off.
  2. Running late? Quick buttons send "I'm up to 15 min late" to the customer or "I'm more than 15 min late" to the office.
  3. "I've arrived": the app checks the driver is within the allowed distance. If not, it says "You are not at the customer location"; "Proceed anyway" is allowed but recorded and reported to admin. Buttons "Msg customer / I'm arrived" and "Msg office / I'm arrived" tell them the driver is there: the first 3 taps send a push (the first also an SMS), after that SMS only, at most once every 2 minutes.

  5. Collect the bags. A proof photo is required; bag and item counts are optional, with a "Missing Information … Proceed Anyway" warning.
  6. "Navigate to Facility" takes the driver to the shop and records the drop-off when the bags arrive (the screen also reminds them the invoice should be finalised within 1 hour of pickup). When the customer is not there

  • The screen shows a "Wait on site" countdown (the zone's grace minutes) and the no-show fee that would apply. "Customer not available" stays locked until the wait is over.

  • Marking a failed attempt needs the phone's location and the driver within the zone's distance of the address ("Move within 100m of the address … to mark failed"). Unlike "I've arrived", there is no "Proceed anyway" here.
  • "Customer not available": pick a reason set by admin, add a note and tick the checklist if the reason needs it, then "Mark as failed".
  • The app confirms "Attempt recorded. Customer will be rescheduled." with the no-show fee recorded or waived. After the maximum collection attempts the booking is cancelled.
  • The driver cannot choose a new time; the customer (or support) reschedules. Until then the driver sees "Waiting for the customer to reschedule — you cannot continue until then."

How an order finds a shop

  1. Preferred shop first

    If admin assigned the customer to a shop, only that shop is tried. Otherwise the shops that completed this customer's earlier orders in the same zone are tried, most recent first. The first one that can take it gets the order alone for the head-start time (default 10 minutes, set in Runtime checks). It sees a countdown; the notification says "A returning customer placed order X. You have 10 minutes to accept."

  2. Everyone in the zone

    When the head-start ends, or the preferred shop declines, every suitable shop in the zone is offered the order at once, nearest first. The shop has 30 minutes to accept.

  3. First accept wins

    The first valid accept gets the order. Every other shop gets "This order was accepted by another agent" and the card disappears; a shop that still tries to accept is told "This order was already taken".

  4. Nobody can take it

    The order waits (the customer is never refused) and stays in admin's Orders → Action Required as "Needs shop assignment". When no shop could even be offered it, admin also gets "Order needs manual assignment" (once per order per day) and the order is held and offered again when shops open or a slot frees up. An offer that simply ran out of time is not sent again. Admin can always assign it by hand.

Declining an order

  • The shop must give a reason (up to 500 characters). A shop can only decline an order that is still waiting, in its own zone, and inside the accept window ("Accept window has expired for this booking").
  • The order is not cancelled. The shop that declined never sees it again, even if it is broadcast later.
  • The preferred shop declines: the head-start ends at once and the order goes to everyone. If nobody else can take it, admin is alerted.
  • A shop in the broadcast declines: the order stays with the other shops until one accepts or the window ends. If every shop declines, no extra alert is sent; the order waits in admin's "Needs shop assignment" list.
  • Admin sees declines and their reasons on the shop page (Rejected tab), but only while the order is still waiting: when any shop accepts it or admin assigns it, all declines for that order are deleted.

Zone boundaries

  • An order only goes to shops whose own address is in the same zone as the customer's address. A shop just across a zone boundary never gets it, however close.
  • Inside the zone there is no distance limit; distance only decides the order of the list (nearest first).
  • A shop cannot accept or decline an order from another zone ("This order is not in your zone").

Customer changes the order before a shop accepts

  • Cancels: the order leaves every shop's New list.
  • Reschedules: the order is sent out again with the new times, but by an older routine: no head-start for the usual shop; shops on hold, excluded, deactivated or that declined are not left out; a shop is skipped if it has any booking at all in that slot (instead of using slot capacity); and shops get a silent update with no "New Booking Request" sound. If no shop fits, the order is held when shops are closed, without an admin alert.
  • After a shop has accepted, a reschedule keeps the same shop, which is notified of the new times.

A customer's first order

  • The customer has no shop history yet, so no shop gets a head-start.
  • The order goes straight to every suitable shop in the zone, nearest first (see Nearest first). Each shop has 30 minutes to accept.
  • The first shop to accept gets the order. From then on that shop is linked to the customer by its work, not by a setting.
  • The same happens for a customer who has ordered before but never had an order completed in this zone.

    The order is sent only to shops in the zone that still have room in both the collection slot and the delivery slot. Those shops get the "New Booking Request — Order X is waiting for acceptance." notification with sound, and the order appears in their New tab, nearest first.

  3. Shop A

    The full shop gets nothing

If it still tries to accept (for example from an old screen), it is refused: "Your pickup slot Thu 22 Oct 10:00–11:00 is full (4 of 4 pickups and deliveries). You can take orders in other slots."

  4. Shop A

    Room frees up

Nearest first

Shops are offered orders, and the shop's New list is sorted, by the shortest total trip: shop → collection address plus shop → delivery address. Ties go to the nearer collection, then the sooner slot, then the newest order. The three nearest collections and deliveries get a "Nearest" badge.

Collection and delivery routes

  • In the shop app, Today and Tomorrow have PICK (collections) and DROP (deliveries) views. Inside each time slot the jobs are listed in shortest order by straight-line distance, starting from the phone's current location (or from the shop when location is off, with a hint to turn it on), as "Stop 1 · 2.3 km", "Stop 2 · …".
  • At collection: "I'm on the way" charges the card hold and lets the customer follow the driver. "I've arrived" checks the driver is at the address. If nobody is there, the driver waits the grace minutes, records a failed attempt and a no-show fee may apply; the customer picks a new time.
  • At delivery: the order must be invoiced and cleaned, and the card balance not stuck in Payment failures. "Out for delivery" turns tracking on again. For cash orders the driver collects the amount due first. A failed delivery asks the customer to choose a new delivery time.

Moving a customer to another shop

Admin can give a customer a preferred shop (Customer → Shops → Find & assign shop, or the shop's Customers tab). Typical reasons:

  • The usual shop gave poor service or the customer complained.
  • The customer asked for a different shop.
  • The usual shop often declines, is often full, or is far away.
  • Spreading work: a new or quiet shop needs customers, or a busy shop needs fewer.

What happens next:

  • New orders go to the new shop first. The old shop loses its head-start even though it has history with the customer; it competes like any other shop.
  • If the new shop cannot take an order (closed, full, on hold), the order goes to everyone in the zone, not back to the old shop first.
  • Orders still waiting for a shop are offered again, new shop first. Orders already accepted stay with their shop; admin sees a list and can move each by hand with Reassign shop.
  • Every change is kept in the customer's routing history, with who did it.
  • Unlink removes the preference: back to the normal rule (the last shop that completed an order, then everyone). Relink makes the shop with the most completed orders the preferred one.

Bad review or bad experience

  1. After delivery the customer rates the shop 1–5 stars. 1–2 stars can only pick negative reasons, 4–5 only positive ones, 3 can pick both, plus a comment.
  2. The shop gets a "New customer review" notification ("Order #X received a 2★ rating"). The rating counts in the shop's average (shop Overview and Reviews tab) and in the Shop Ratings report, which shows each shop's share of low ratings and the top reasons.
  3. A bad review does not block the shop by itself. Orders keep following the normal rules until admin decides.
  4. If admin decides the customer should not use that shop again, admin excludes the shop for this customer (Customer → Shops → Exclude, with a reason such as "rude driver" or "damaged items").

After an exclusion:

  • The excluded shop never gets this customer's new orders: no head-start and no marketplace offer. If it tries to accept, it is told "This customer is excluded from your shop and cannot be accepted via marketplace."
  • Orders still waiting are taken away from that shop. Orders it already accepted are listed for admin to move by hand.
  • Who gets the orders instead: the customer's preferred shop if admin set one; otherwise the next shop in the customer's history; otherwise every other suitable shop in the zone, nearest first. If no shop takes it, admin is alerted and assigns a shop.
  • Admin can still assign the excluded shop by hand, but is asked first: "This customer is excluded from … (bad experience). Assign anyway?"
  • Include lifts the exclusion; the shop can get this customer's new orders again (orders already handed to others are not offered back).
Possible next stepToday admin learns about a 1–2 star review from the shop page or the Shop Ratings report. An alert to admin on every low rating, with an "Exclude this shop" button, can be added.

Promotions, campaigns and credit

The four building blocks

Promotion

The offer: what the customer gets, where, for whom, when, and whether it is automatic or needs a code.

Campaign

A folder with a budget for several promotions (for example "Autumn 2026, £500"). When the budget is used up, its promotions stop.

Coupon code

Unlocks a "Coupon Code Required" promotion. One code per booking. Codes are unique across the whole system. Code checks are limited to 10 tries in 10 minutes on the code-check service, but the checkout's "apply coupon" step has no limit yet.

Banner

Home screen advert. A banner linked to a promotion shows the offer text and hides itself when the promotion stops.

How a promotion reaches the bill

  1. Published

    Admin creates it as a draft and publishes it. It becomes Active (or Scheduled until its start date). Dates and times are UK time.

  2. Seen

    Automatic public offers appear under "Offers for you" on the home screen and "Offers on this order" at checkout. Code offers are never listed; the customer must know the code.

  3. Held at booking

    Checked when the customer books: zone, day and time, first order, order count, customer type, payment method, limits, campaign budget and the code. Every promotion that passes is held on the order. "Pay now" does not change.

  4. Priced on the invoice

    When the shop prices the items: minimum spend, items, caps and stacking are applied. The customer's history and the clock are taken from booking time, so an offer that ended after booking is still honoured. One that admin paused is dropped.

  5. Locked when paid

    Paid orders keep their discount for good; later invoice edits never change it. Uses and campaign budget are counted now. Holds that gave nothing are released and do not count.

The rules that decide the amount

  • Never more than is still to pay. A discount cannot exceed what is left after "Pay now". Nothing paid at booking is refunded. See the bill example in Money.
  • A discount on an item can never exceed the item's price. "£50 off each item" on a £20 item takes £20 off.
  • Cap: a percentage offer can have a "Max Discount Cap". 20% up to £10: a £30 basket saves £6, an £80 basket saves £10. Customers see "20% OFF (up to £10.00)".
  • "£ off each item" is per piece, not per order. £30 off each item on £2.95 shirts makes every shirt free. To give a fixed amount in total (for example £30 off Dry Clean and Wash & Iron altogether), also set Max Discount Cap to £30. The wizard shows the cap for item, category and service discounts in £ too, and says "off every single item … at most £30.00 in total".
  • "First order only" in Audience means each customer can get the offer once, on their first order (no earlier order that was not cancelled), whatever "Uses per Customer", "per Day" or "per Week" say. The wizard summary shows "First order only · Once per customer (their first order)" and warns on the Limits step.
  • Service fee is never discounted. Delivery is free today, so Free Delivery and Delivery Discount offers save £0 for now.
  • Stacking: one delivery offer; item offers in priority order; then one basket offer on what is left, unless all basket offers are marked "Stackable"; one cashback. Higher priority wins; equal priority goes to the older promotion. Offers in the same "Stack Group" never combine.
  • Basket offers cover the whole basket. A "Basket contains service" condition only decides who gets the offer. To discount only some services, use Service Discount, Category Discount or Item Discount.
  • The platform pays for discounts. The shop's share is on the price before discount.

Cashback and customer credit

  • Cashback is added to the customer's credit once the order is paid and delivered. The customer gets a "Cashback added" notification.
  • Credit is used automatically on the next invoice, after promotions, never more than is left to pay. Credit bigger than the bill leaves the rest in the balance.
  • Credit expires 365 days after it was earned; the oldest is used first. It is never paid out as cash.
  • Admin can add or remove credit (up to £1,000 at a time, with a reason) from the customer's Credit tab.
  • Cancelled order: credit used on it goes back. Full refund: unspent cashback from it is taken back, credit spent on it is returned. Partial refund: credit does not change.

Campaign budgets

  • The budget counts discounts and cashback on paid orders. When it reaches 100% the campaign's promotions stop ("This offer is no longer available"). A refund gives the budget back.
  • The last order before the limit can take the budget slightly over, because spend is counted when orders are paid.
  • Pausing or completing a campaign stops all its promotions. A draft campaign does not stop them.
  • The budget is checked again when the invoice is priced. An offer held at booking is dropped if the budget ran out in between.

Repeat (recurring) orders

  • At checkout the customer picks Just once, Weekly (every 7 days), Every two weeks (14) or Every four weeks (28).
  • The plan starts only after the first booking succeeds (a declined card leaves no plan).
  • The next order is created by itself one interval after the previous order was placed: same addresses, slot times, services (priced from today's catalogue), preferences, payment method, instructions and driver tip. If that date has already passed, it moves forward to the next free interval.
  • It is offered to shops like any new order (preferred shop first). The customer gets "Next order booked — Your Weekly order is booked for pickup Wed 14 Oct at 10:00. You can change or skip it in your orders."
  • The customer can pause, resume or turn off the plan in the app. Admin can do the same and is the only one who can change the frequency. Cancelling one order does not stop the plan; the app says so.
  • After 3 failed attempts in a row (Runtime checks, 1–10) the plan pauses itself. After a failure the next try is a full interval later, so 3 failures can mean 3 missed cycles. A blocked customer or a missing source order pauses the plan at once.

Common questions

Why does the customer see no discount at checkout?

Prices are only known after the shop counts the items. The offer is saved on the order and taken off the invoice. "Pay now" never changes because of an offer.

"I entered a coupon code but it was not applied."

Most often the customer set or changed the driver tip after entering the code: changing the tip removes the code without a message. Check the order's Promotions card; if no code is there, it was not saved at booking.

"You promised 20% but I got £10 off."

The offer had a cap and said so: "20% OFF (up to £10.00)". 20% of that basket was more than £10, so £10 was taken off.

"£30 off" on two services took off more than £30.

The offer was "£ off each item", which is per piece: every cheap item became free, limited only by what was still to pay. For £30 in total, set Max Discount Cap to £30 (or use 100% with a £30 cap).

"£50 off each item" but the items do not show £0.

Each item can only lose its own price, and the whole discount can only take off what was still to pay after "Pay now" (minimum order + service fee + tip). The allowed discount is shared across the items, so each shows a small remaining amount. Together those amounts equal what was paid at booking.

A first-order offer did not apply.

The customer already had an earlier order that was not cancelled (any status counts), or the order was placed before the offer was published, or outside its zone, days or times.

A basket offer limited to two services discounted everything.

"Basket contains service" only decides who qualifies; Basket Discount always covers the whole basket. Use Service Discount with those two services as the targets.

A customer gave a shop 1 star. Will their next order still go to that shop?

Yes, until admin acts. A bad review only notifies the shop and shows in the Shop Ratings report. To stop it, admin excludes that shop for the customer (Customer → Shops → Exclude). The orders then go to the preferred shop, the next shop in the customer's history, or everyone else in the zone. See Bad review or bad experience.

Why does the old shop no longer get this customer's orders first?

Admin gave the customer a preferred shop. From then on only that shop gets the head-start; the old shop competes with every other shop. See Moving a customer to another shop.

When is a customer "returning"?

For the head-start, after a shop completes 1 order for them in the zone. The "Returning" badge in admin needs 2 completed orders at that shop. See When a customer becomes "returning".

A customer booked at night. When do shops see it?

The order is held and sent when a shop in the zone opens or opens its app. Shops can accept it until 1 hour after the platform opens; after that admin assigns it. See Business hours.

Our shop is open but did not get an order in our area.

Check the collection slot: it must be inside your business hours on the collection day. A Saturday collection is never offered to a shop whose Saturday is a day off.

A shop is closed. Can it still deliver?

Yes. Once a shop has accepted an order, collecting and delivering are not limited by its hours. Only new offers depend on hours.

A shop says it cannot accept an order.

Read the message in the shop app: another shop still has the head-start, the slot is full, the shop is on hold or deactivated, the customer is excluded, or another shop accepted first. Admin can still assign the order by hand.

A shop says it heard no sound for a new order that other shops got.

Its slot was probably full. With slot capacity on, a shop that is full for the order's collection or delivery slot is left out of the broadcast: no notification, no sound, and the order is not in its New tab. The banner on the New tab shows which slots are full. See When a shop's slot is full.

A shop took more orders in one hour than we wanted.

Turn on "Use slot capacity for all shops" in Runtime checks and set the number (blank = 4). While it is off there is no limit.

An order is waiting and no shop takes it.

Admin receives "Order needs manual assignment" with the reason (no shop offers the services, every shop is full for the slot, or shops declined / are closed / are excluded). If shops were offered it but nobody accepted within 30 minutes, there is no alert: check Orders → Action Required → "Needs shop assignment". Use Assign shop on the order.

The card balance did not go through.

Temporary declines are retried (up to 3 tries); a hard decline such as an expired card goes straight to Orders → Payment failures. Admin can switch the balance to cash, let the order continue to delivery anyway, or keep waiting (which stops the automatic retries). Often the quickest fix is the customer adding a new card in the app: every open order moves to it and is charged again straight away, as long as not all 3 automatic tries are used up.

A customer missed three collections. Do they get their money back?

Not automatically. After 3 failed collections the order is cancelled and the "Pay now" amount stays charged (plus any no-show fees). Admin decides whether to refund from the order page.

Why can't the customer cancel any more?

Customers can cancel only until the invoice is generated. After that, support can help (for example a refund from the order page).

How do we stop all promotions quickly?

Pause the promotion, or pause its campaign. For an emergency stop of every promotion at checkout, the technical team can switch the promotions feature off; bills then go back to the old behaviour.

Can a customer change how often a repeat order comes?

Not in the app. They can pause or turn it off; admin changes the frequency by editing the order.

How do we say sorry to one customer?

Customer page → Credit tab → Add credit with a reason. It is used on their next invoice.
